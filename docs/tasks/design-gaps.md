# Design Gaps Found During Task Planning

**Phase:** 3 — TASK · **Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** Open — reported, not resolved

Per the Phase 3 rules, these gaps are **reported, not silently fixed**. Each lists the smallest proposed correction. A correction that changes product behaviour or architecture needs approval (PO or tech lead) and must then be recorded in the relevant `docs/design/` document (or `open-decisions.md`) by the design owner — not by a task-executing agent.
